Mengzhou IVA — external dependencies and derived configuration

Props/Controls.cfg, Props/MFD_*.cfg and Props/Seats.cfg contain configurations derived
from ASET Consolidated Props Pack by Alexustas, adopted by Stone Blue.
These derived configuration files are provided under the same Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License as ASET Props.
Changes: private KIU_MZ_* names, explicit external MODEL references, selected
pages, per-station default pages and refresh rates, and a cabin-light binding.
Seats.cfg references the original SeatV2 model with a private static PROP name;
its cockpit-global position animations and sounds are intentionally omitted.
Seven InternalSeat modules remain owned by the Mengzhou INTERNAL definition.
The original ASET models, textures, fonts and sounds remain external dependencies.

RasterPropMonitor and FreeIVA are external plugin dependencies; no DLLs are
included with this IVA. Stock fallback instruments and seven Seat_Passenger chair models are referenced
through KSP; no stock chair or Kerbal meshes are bundled in the exported MUs.
Stock Kerbal meshes used during local fit review are excluded from the runtime
and are only used as external references for local authoring and review.

Spaces/MZWhite.dds is an exact, 152-byte loading alias of the user-maintained
KIU/Common/KIU_Common_texture/Shared_White.v3.3.0.dds. MODEL texture mappings bind
it to that public library. It contains no new artwork; the alias is required by
KSP's model-directory texture loading before remapping.
